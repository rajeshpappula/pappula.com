Where it begins

A village at the heart of our story.

Pappulavaripalem is where the Pappula story has deep roots. With around 90% of people there sharing the surname, the name is part of the village's everyday identity.

Over time, people from the village have settled in places across the world. Their paths are different, but the connection to a shared hometown and family name remains.
